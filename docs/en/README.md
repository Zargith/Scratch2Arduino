## Tutorials

Eight projects, from the simplest to the most complete. Each one matches an example of the gallery
(**New** in the application), with its wiring diagram.

| # | Tutorial | Parts | What you learn | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Blink](tutorials/01-blink.md) | the board alone | start block, output, wait, forever, upload, Live | 20 min |
| 2 | [Traffic light](tutorials/02-traffic-light.md) | 3 LEDs, 3 resistors | wiring an LED, sequence, functions | 45 min |
| 3 | [Button and LED](tutorials/03-button-led.md) | button, LED, resistor | input, if … then … else, variable | 40 min |
| 4 | [Potentiometer and servo](tutorials/04-servo-potentiometer.md) | potentiometer, SG90 servo | analog input, variable, map | 45 min |
| 5 | [Distance alarm](tutorials/05-distance-alarm.md) | HC-SR04, RGB LED, buzzer | sensor, threshold, computed duration | 1 h |
| 6 | [Thermometer](tutorials/06-thermometer.md) | DHT11, LCD screen | digital sensor, screen, text and numbers | 1 h |
| 7 | [Reaction game](tutorials/07-reaction-game.md) | LED matrix, OLED, button, buzzer | randomness, waiting for an event, score | 1 h 30 |
| 8 | [Remote robot](tutorials/08-remote-robot.md) | ESP32, L298N, 2 motors, phone | motors, Bluetooth, functions | 2 h |

More: [block catalog](../block-catalog.md), [examples and wiring diagrams](../../examples/README.md),
